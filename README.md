# Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Superstore retail sales dataset using Python. It was developed in the context of the OASIS INFOBYTE Data Analytics Internship.

The objective is to analyze sales trends, product performance, customer segments, regional sales, and profitability to identify useful business insights. The project includes data cleaning, statistical analysis, data aggregation, and visualizations.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall retail sales and profit.
* Identify monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales trends.
* Find the best-selling products by quantity sold.
* Analyze revenue generated by product categories.
* Understand sales across customer segments.
* Compare sales performance across regions.
* Study profit by category and year.
* Explore the relationship between sales and profit.
* Analyze the relationship between discounts and profitability.
* Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

## 📁 Dataset

The notebook loads the Superstore dataset using the filename `superstore final.csv`.

The analysis uses fields such as Order Date, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Order ID, Customer ID, Segment, Product Name, Category, Region, and Discount.

**Note:** The notebook expects the CSV file to be present in the working directory. Include the dataset in the repository only if its source and usage terms permit redistribution.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. Import the required Python libraries.
2. Load the Superstore dataset.
3. Inspect the first and last rows, dimensions, columns, and data types.
4. Check missing values and duplicate records.
5. Remove duplicate rows.
6. Calculate descriptive statistics.
7. Convert Order Date into datetime format.
8. Create Year, Month, Month Name, and Quarter columns.
9. Calculate total sales, total profit, quantity sold, and unique orders.
10. Analyze monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales.
11. Examine customer segments and product performance.
12. Analyze category-wise and regional sales.
13. Explore profit, discount, and numerical correlations.
14. Summarize key business insights and recommendations.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis is designed to identify:

* Periods with higher and lower sales.
* Products with the highest sales quantities.
* Categories generating the most revenue.
* Customer segments contributing the most sales.
* Regions with stronger sales performance.
* Relationships between sales, profit, and quantity.
* Whether higher discounts are associated with lower profitability.

Specific winning months, products, categories, and regions should be reported using the notebook's actual executed results.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

1. **Inventory Planning:** Maintain sufficient stock for high-demand products.
2. **Category Performance:** Monitor high-revenue and low-performing categories.
3. **Seasonal Marketing:** Plan promotions around lower-sales periods.
4. **Discount Optimization:** Evaluate discount strategies with profitability in mind.
5. **Customer Targeting:** Develop campaigns for important customer segments.
6. **Regional Strategy:** Study successful regions and identify improvement opportunities elsewhere.
